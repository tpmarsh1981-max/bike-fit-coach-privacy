Bike Fit Coach privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026.

Bike Fit Coach is a bike fit app. It is built so that your information never leaves your phone.

What Bike Fit Coach stores

The photos you take or choose, the joint points you mark, your riding style, inseam, flexibility answer, comfort notes, saved fits and reminder settings. All of it is stored only on your device, inside the app.

What Bike Fit Coach sends

Nothing. Bike Fit Coach has no account, no server, no advertising and no analytics. It does not share or sell any data. If you use “Share report”, the text goes only to the app or person you choose.

Camera, photos and notifications

Bike Fit Coach asks for camera or photo access only when you take or choose a photo, and asks to send notifications only if you turn on the daily reminder. You can change these in your phone’s settings at any time.

Deleting your data

Use “Delete all my data” in About, privacy and safety, or delete the app.

Children

Bike Fit Coach is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact
